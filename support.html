It helps to say which device you are using, your iOS or iPadOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Traitors runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and there are no ads or in-app purchases.

Who are the other players?

The seven other players are controlled by the game. Their names and occupations are fictional.

Where is my progress kept?

Your saved game, statistics and settings are stored on your device only. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Which devices are supported?

Traitors runs on iPhone and iPad, and supports VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.
